3. Calibrate physical latency

Video lead: positive values advance the picture relative to audio. Increase it when a sound is heard before the matching picture. Decrease it when sound is heard late.

Saved across launches. Large changes converge slowly during playback; use Resynchronize to apply them immediately. Small video-speed changes never affect the audio bitstream.
